Piñata world

The knight, rogue, dwarf and wizard live on this page. The cards and tiles are their ground: they walk along them, step up small rises and drop from one to the one below, by motion capture matched live and kept on the surface, every frame planned and drawn in your browser by the Piñata motion engine.

Any element is ground

Mark an element and its top edge becomes a surface; mark it a wall and its sides block the way.

.card { --pinata: ground; }

Point at something

Rest the pointer on a button, or move keyboard focus to it, and the nearest character that can reach it walks over.

Words and favourite places

data-pinata-say gives a place words; --pinata-attract makes characters linger. The sunny tile below is a favourite.

Poke

Click a character to turn it round. Clicks anywhere else reach the page as usual.
