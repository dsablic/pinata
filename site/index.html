Piñata world

A cast of characters living on procedurally generated terrain: they wander, look around, visit each other for a chat, dash about, and rest; capes and tails stream in the wind. Click one to startle it. Every footstep, ramp and stair, every frame, is planned and rendered live in your browser by the Piñata motion engine; the world is one declarative config.
